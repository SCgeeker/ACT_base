## ⚡ Identified Tension

**Framework A**: Embodied Simulation Theory [@Smith-2020]
- **Claim**: [[Smith-2020-005|Perceptual-motor simulation is *necessary*]] for conceptual processing
- **Evidence**: [[Smith-2020-012|Motor disruption impairs comprehension]]

**Framework B**: Linguistic Association Model [@Jones-2021]
- **Claim**: [[Jones-2021-010|Linguistic association is *sufficient*]]
- **Evidence**: [[Jones-2021-018|Symbolic priming works without motor activation]]

**Core Conflict**:
- Necessity claim (A) vs. Sufficiency claim (B)
- Both have empirical support
- Theories make different predictions about motor area involvement

**Tension Severity**: **High** (fundamental theoretical disagreement)

## 🔧 Reconciliation Strategy: Domain Partitioning

### Reconciliation Logic

The apparent conflict can be resolved by recognizing that **both theories are correct within their respective domains**:

- **Theory A (Embodied Simulation)** applies to:
  - [[Smith-2020-008|Action verbs]] (e.g., "kick", "grasp")
  - [[Smith-2020-015|Concrete concepts]] with strong sensorimotor grounding
  - **Domain Characteristics**: High imageability, strong motor associations

- **Theory B (Linguistic Association)** applies to:
  - [[Jones-2021-020|Abstract concepts]] (e.g., "justice", "freedom")
  - [[Jones-2021-025|Linguistic-context-rich scenarios]]
  - **Domain Characteristics**: Low imageability, symbolic relationships dominant

### Supporting Evidence for Partitioning

**Concreteness Effects**:
- [[Chen-2022-010|fMRI meta-analysis]]: Motor areas activated more for concrete > abstract words
- [[Wang-2023-015|Behavioral study]]: Motor disruption affects concrete (not abstract) word processing

**Context Modulation**:
- [[Lee-2021-018|Priming study]]: Rich linguistic context reduces embodiment effects
- [[Martinez-2022-020|Reading task]]: Motor involvement decreases with contextual support

## 🧪 Testable Predictions

### Prediction 1: Motor Disruption Differential Effect
**Design**:
- **IV**: Motor cortex TMS (disruption) vs. Sham
- **DV**: Comprehension accuracy
- **Stimuli**: Action verbs (concrete) vs. Abstract verbs

**Predicted Outcome**:
- TMS disrupts: Action verb comprehension > Abstract verb comprehension
- Interaction: TMS × Word Type (p < .05)

**Adjudicates**: If confirmed → supports domain partitioning

---

### Prediction 2: Concreteness × Context Interaction
**Design**:
- **IV1**: Word Concreteness (High vs. Low)
- **IV2**: Linguistic Context (Rich vs. Minimal)
- **DV**: Motor area activation (fMRI)

**Predicted Outcome**:
- Highest motor activation: High concreteness × Minimal context
- Lowest motor activation: Low concreteness × Rich context
- 2-way interaction (p < .05)

**Adjudicates**: If confirmed → supports domain + context constraints

## 其他策略的輸出重點

- 明確列出 Prediction A vs. Prediction B 的不同預期結果

- Integrated Model 函數形式：`Embodiment strength = f(concreteness, context, expertise)`
