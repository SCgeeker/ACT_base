# 字符级语言模型

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "We’ll train RNN character-level language models. That is, we’ll give the RNN a huge chunk of text and ask it to model the probability distribution of the next character..."

通过预测文本序列中下一个字符的概率分布，让 RNN 学习语言的规律并生成新文本。
