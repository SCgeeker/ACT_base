# 输入历史的影响

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "crucially this output vector’s contents are influenced not only by the input you just fed in, but also on the entire history of inputs you’ve fed in in the past."

RNN 的输出不仅取决于当前的输入，还高度依赖于之前所有输入历史积累的状态。
