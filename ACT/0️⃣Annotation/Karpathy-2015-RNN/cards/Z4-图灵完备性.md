# 图灵完备性

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "In fact, it is known that RNNs are Turing-Complete in the sense that they can to simulate arbitrary programs (with proper weights)."

RNN 在理论上具有模拟任何计算程序的能力，其本质可以被视为描述程序的一种方式。
