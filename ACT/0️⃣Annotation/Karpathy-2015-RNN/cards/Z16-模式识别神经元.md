# 模式识别神经元

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "Different neurons might be looking for different patterns; We found some that identify whether we are inside or outside of a quote."

某些特定的神经元会自动演化出识别特定文本模式的功能，如括号匹配或是否处于引号内。
