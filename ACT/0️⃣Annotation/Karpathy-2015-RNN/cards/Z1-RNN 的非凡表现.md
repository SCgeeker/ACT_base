# RNN 的非凡表现

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "Sometimes the ratio of how simple your model is to the quality of the results you get out of it blows past your expectations."

描述了 RNN 在模型结构相对简单的情况下，能够生成高质量、接近有意义序列的惊人能力。
