# Gates  門控機制

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "The LSTM does have the ability to remove or add information to the cell state, carefully regulated by structures called gates."

Gates are mechanisms to optionally let information through, controlling what is added or removed. 
門控是選擇性讓資訊通過的機制，精確調節細胞狀態中資訊的增刪。
