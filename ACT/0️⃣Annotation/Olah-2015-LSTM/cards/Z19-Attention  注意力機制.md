# Attention  注意力機制

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "The next step is attention! The idea is to let every step of an RNN pick information to look at from some larger collection."

A future innovation that allows models to dynamically focus on specific parts of a large information set. 
之後的技術創新，允許模型動態地從龐大的資訊集中挑選重點關注。
