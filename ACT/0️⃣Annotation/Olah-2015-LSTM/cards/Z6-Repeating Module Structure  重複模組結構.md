# Repeating Module Structure  重複模組結構

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "All recurrent neural networks have the form of a chain of repeating modules of neural network."

RNNs consist of repeating units; in standard RNNs, this is usually a single simple layer like tanh. 
所有 RNN 都由重複的神經網路模組組成；在標準 RNN 中，這通常只是單一的 tanh 層。
