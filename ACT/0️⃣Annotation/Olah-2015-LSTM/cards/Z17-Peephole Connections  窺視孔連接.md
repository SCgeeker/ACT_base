# Peephole Connections  窺視孔連接

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "Peephole Connections: Let the gate layers look at the cell state."

A variant where gates are directly influenced by the cell state, not just the hidden state. 
一種變體，允許門控層直接觀察細胞狀態，而不僅僅是隱藏狀態。
