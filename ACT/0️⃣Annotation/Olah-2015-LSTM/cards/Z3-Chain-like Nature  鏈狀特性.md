# Chain-like Nature  鏈狀特性

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "This chain-like nature reveals that recurrent neural networks are intimately related to sequences and lists."

The unrolled structure of an RNN shows it is the natural architecture for sequential data. 
RNN 的展開結構揭示了它是處理序列和列表數據的自然架構。
