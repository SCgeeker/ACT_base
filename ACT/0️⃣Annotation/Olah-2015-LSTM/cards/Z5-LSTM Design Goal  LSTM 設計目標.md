# LSTM Design Goal  LSTM 設計目標

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "LSTMs are explicitly designed to avoid the long-term dependency problem."

Remembering information for long periods is the default behavior of LSTMs, not something they struggle to learn. 
LSTM 被專門設計用來避免長期依賴問題，長期記憶是其預設行為而非學習負擔。
