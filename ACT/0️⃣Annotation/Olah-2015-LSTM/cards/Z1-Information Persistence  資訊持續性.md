# Information Persistence  資訊持續性

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "Humans don’t start their thinking from scratch every second... Your thoughts have persistence."

Humans understand things based on previous context; RNNs address this by allowing information to persist. 
人類並非每秒都從頭開始思考，想法具有連續性；RNN 透過允許資訊持久化來解決此需求。
