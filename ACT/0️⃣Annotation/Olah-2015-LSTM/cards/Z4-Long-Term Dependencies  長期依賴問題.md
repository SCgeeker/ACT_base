# Long-Term Dependencies  長期依賴問題

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "Unfortunately, as the gap grows, RNNs become unable to learn to connect the information."

Although capable in theory, RNNs struggle in practice to connect information separated by large gaps. 
理論上可行，但實踐中 RNN 難以學習並連接間隔較遠的資訊。
