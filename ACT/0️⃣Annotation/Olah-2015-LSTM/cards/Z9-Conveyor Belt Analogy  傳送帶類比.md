# Convey Belt Analogy  傳送帶類比

## 📝 核心概念 (Core Concept)
> "The cell state is kind of like a conveyor belt... It’s very easy for information to just flow along it unchanged."

The cell state allows information to flow through the steps with minimal modifications. 
細胞狀態就像一條傳送帶，讓資訊能夠在各個步驟間幾乎不變地流動。
